Choosing the right motor

Not sure what you need? These are the questions we ask buyers first. Send us the answers and we'll suggest motors from our stock.

  • Your boat. Its length, hull type and the maximum horsepower on its capacity plate.
  • Shaft length. Measure from the top of the transom to the bottom of the hull: about 15" is a short shaft, 20" long and 25" extra-long.
  • Two-stroke or four-stroke. Two-strokes are lighter and simpler; four-strokes are quieter and use less fuel, with no oil to mix.
  • Controls. Whether you steer with a tiller or have remote steering and throttle at a console.
  • How you'll use it. Fishing, cruising, towing or offshore running all point to different power ranges.
