Financing

Financing is available

A good used outboard shouldn't have to wait until you can pay for it all at once. Financing is available, and we arrange it individually around the motor you choose.

Financing isn't set up through this website. Mention it in your enquiry or order request and we'll talk through the options with you directly: what's possible for that motor, what we'd need from you, and how the payments would work. Asking costs nothing and doesn't commit you to anything.

How it works

Four steps to your motor

  1. 01

    Choose a motor

    Find the motor you want in our stock, or tell us what you're looking for.

  2. 02

    Ask about financing

    Send an enquiry or order request and say you'd like to finance it.

  3. 03

    Agree the terms

    We go through the options with you and agree a plan that works for both sides, in writing.

  4. 04

    Crated and shipped

    Once the plan is agreed, the motor is crated and sent by freight as arranged.

What to have ready

It speeds things up if you include these when you get in touch:

  • The motor you're interested in, or the horsepower and shaft length you need.
  • Your budget, including how much you'd like to pay up front.
  • Your delivery ZIP code, so freight can be included in the total.
  • How you'd prefer to pay the up-front part and any later payments.
  • The best way to reach you: phone, text, WhatsApp or email.

Budget for the whole job

When you work out what you can afford, it helps to include more than the motor itself:

  • Freight to your address, which we quote for your ZIP code.
  • Rigging: controls, cables, gauges and fuel lines if your boat doesn't already have the right ones.
  • Propeller sized for your boat and the way you use it.
  • Installation by a local marine mechanic, if you're not fitting it yourself.

Questions

Financing FAQ

Does asking about financing commit me to anything?

No. You can ask about any motor and decide afterwards. Nothing is agreed until we've both confirmed the terms.

Can I put down a deposit to hold a motor?

Ask us about the motor you want. We'll tell you whether we can hold it and on what terms.

Can I finance more than one motor, such as a twin set?

Yes, you can ask about financing for several motors in the same enquiry.

Can freight be included?

Freight is quoted for your ZIP code and can be included in the total we agree with you.

How quickly can it be arranged?

The sooner we have the details listed above, the sooner we can confirm the options. We reply by your preferred contact method.
