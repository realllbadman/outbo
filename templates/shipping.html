What affects the freight cost

  • Size and weight. A 40 HP tiller motor costs less to move than a 250 HP V6 with a 25" shaft.
  • Distance. Neighbouring states usually cost less than coast to coast.
  • Type of address. Business addresses with a loading dock are often cheaper than homes.
  • Liftgate. If there's no dock or forklift where you are, the truck needs a liftgate to lower the crate.

Delivery options

  • To your home with liftgate service, so the crate is lowered to the ground.
  • To a business such as a marina, boat yard or workshop with a dock or forklift.
  • Terminal pickup. Collect the crate from the carrier's nearest depot. This is often the cheapest option.
  • Local pickup in {{ biz.business_location }}, by arrangement.

When your motor arrives

  1. Look over the crate before the driver leaves.
  2. If anything looks damaged, write it on the delivery receipt before you sign, and take photos.
  3. Unpack the motor and check it over.
  4. Contact us straight away by WhatsApp, phone or email if anything isn't right.

Questions

Shipping FAQ

How long does delivery take?

It depends on the distance and the carrier's schedule. We give you an estimate with your freight quote, and the carrier calls ahead to set a delivery time.

Do you ship outside the United States?

Our standard service covers all 50 states. If you're elsewhere, send us a message with your location and we'll tell you what's possible.

Can I pick the motor up myself?

Yes. Local pickup in {{ biz.business_location }} can be arranged. Let us know when you enquire.

Can I ship more than one motor together?

Yes. Add them all to your cart or list them in your enquiry, and we'll quote freight for the whole shipment.

Is the shipment insured?

Ask us about freight insurance when you get your quote, and we'll include the options for your shipment.

Do I need to be home for delivery?

Someone needs to be there to check the crate and sign for it. The carrier contacts you beforehand to agree on a time.
